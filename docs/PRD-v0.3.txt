  Part A — Summary
  The Mahagathe app gives one place for the teachings, practice and activities of the
  Mahagathe Foundation Public Charitable Trust. The app is small at launch. It grows one
  vibhaga at a time.

  Why the app is different. Ven. Mahāmuni Sritha Mahāgatha is the Seventieth
  Mahādhibhū of the Rudra Sampradāya of the Mahāpāshupata mata. The app shows this
  living lineage first. Each teaching in the app has a verified source.

  Who uses the app:

        Sādhakas who practise Samatha and the 40-Day Mandala.
        Students and young people in Yuva Chetana and Vidya classes.
        Parents of Bala Chetana children.
        Devotees in India and abroad who want daily guidance.
        Volunteers and donors.

  The daily reason to open the app: Samatha practice and the 40-Day Mandala. The daily
  Guru Vākyam supports this habit.

  What V1 contains:

     1. A daily Guru Vākyam, as text.
     2. One free introductory Samatha practice for all users.
     3. Samatha and Dhyānbāri resources for enrolled sādhakas.
     4. The 40-Day Mandala with a group count.
     5. The Parampara page.
     6. Mahabharata episodes as text, written by Mahagathe from the BORI critical edition.
     7. An events calendar with registration.
     8. A hub page for each vibhaga.
     9. Donations by UPI.

  What V1 does not contain: video in the app, a pañcāṅ ga, audio Guru Vākyams, rankings, a
  parent view and games. Videos stay on YouTube. The app links to them.

  Content and authenticity rules
  These rules apply to all content in the app. The CMS must enforce them before an item
  can be published.

  C-1 Approval. The content authority (D-21) approves all teachings, practice instructions
  and lineage information. An editor cannot approve their own work.

  C-2 Sources. Each item records its source, its edition and its review date.

        Mahabharata: the BORI critical edition only. Do not use other recensions or folk
        additions.
        Ramayana: the Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa critical edition (Oriental Institute, Baroda).
        "Vedic" content: the Saṃhitās, Āraṇyakas or Upaniṣads only, after the team defines
        the lesson scope (D-19).

  C-3 Own words. Mahagathe writes its own Itihasa text. The steps are:

     1. A writer reads the BORI adhyāyas. The writer can use the Debroy translation to
        understand the text, but does not copy from it.
     2. The writer writes the episode in their own words.
     3. The writer tags each section with its range, for example Ādi 1.1–15.
     4. A second reviewer compares the episode with the critical edition.

  Get legal advice before the app quotes ślokas or uses translated text.

  C-4 Teaching and paraphrase. The app shows a verbatim teaching and a paraphrase
  differently. It shows the Mahamuni's name only when the source is verified.

  C-5 Names and spelling. Use IAST in Roman script. Use the approved spellings in D-13.
  Write the Mahamuni's name and titles in the form that the content authority approves.

  C-6 Format. V1 content is text and approved practice audio only. Videos stay on YouTube.
  Do not machine-translate scripture and show it as a verified teaching.

  C-7 No claims. Do not promise health, spiritual or material results from a practice, a
  product or a donation.

  C-8 Content plan. Each content type has a named owner and a monthly quantity. For
  launch, the team needs about 90 Guru Vākyams, 1 free practice and 10 Mahabharata
  episodes. The about 200 posts in the Wisdom section of the website are the first source
  for Guru Vākyams.

  Technical and non-functional requirements

  Stack
        App: React Native, one codebase for Android and iOS (D-01). Use over-the-air updates
        for small fixes, so that the team does not need a store release for each one.
        Backend: a managed backend with low running cost. It handles accounts,
        entitlements, Mandala records and payments. The server enforces all access rules.
        Content: a headless CMS with the workflow in A-01 to A-08.
        Payments: an Indian payment provider that supports UPI. The app never stores card
        data.
        Notifications: push notifications. WhatsApp reminders for events are a possible later
        option.

  Standard checks for every screen
  These 10 checks replace the cross-cutting IDs -11 to -20 of the v0.2 register. QA applies
  them to each screen, API and data item.

     1. Access: an unauthorised user cannot open gated actions or private data.
     2. Loading: the screen shows a loading state during slow operations.
     3. Empty: an empty screen explains why it is empty and gives a next step.
     4. Failure: a failure shows a clear message and a safe retry, with no duplicate writes.
     5. Accessibility: labels, focus order, contrast, text size and touch targets work.
     6. Layout: no clipped text and no side scroll at supported sizes.
     7. Privacy: the screen shows and logs only necessary data.
     8. Analytics: events use approved names, need consent and contain no private data.
     9. Lifecycle: draft, archived and withdrawn content never shows to the public.
  10. Tests: positive, negative, boundary and recovery tests link to the requirement ID.

  The full engineering lists from v0.2 (MAH-54 to MAH-62) stay valid as the build standard.
  See the traceability table.
